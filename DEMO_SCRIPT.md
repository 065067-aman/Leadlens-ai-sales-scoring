# 2–3 Minute Demo Script

## 0:00–0:15 — Problem
“Sales teams receive many leads but cannot give every lead the same attention. LeadLens AI prioritizes leads using firmographic and engagement signals.”

## 0:15–0:35 — Dashboard
Show total leads, Hot/Warm/Cold distribution and the priority queue.
Say: “The ML model converts the signals into a 0–100 score and a Hot, Warm or Cold tier.”

## 0:35–1:10 — Score a Lead
Enter a strong lead:
Enterprise, SaaS, Referral, Delhi NCR, 10 website visits, 8 opens, 4 downloads, Demo Yes, 4 calls, 3 days, ₹750000.
Click Calculate.
Show score, tier and score-driver table.

## 1:10–1:45 — AI Sales Recommendation
Click the AI recommendation / Sales Copilot.
Say: “Gemini does not decide the score. It explains the model output and recommends the next action using only the supplied facts.”

## 1:45–2:10 — Batch Scoring
Open Batch Scoring. Upload sample_leads.csv. Show scored table and download button.
Say: “A sales manager can score an entire CRM export and download a CRM-ready file.”

## 2:10–2:35 — Edge Case / Failure Mode
Upload a CSV with a missing column or explain the validation message.
Then open Model & Limitations.
Say: “The prototype also has explicit failure handling. If Gemini is unavailable, scoring still works. The model is trained on synthetic data, so it should not be treated as production truth.”

## 2:35–2:50 — Close
“LeadLens AI combines predictive analytics, explainable AI and practical sales workflow in one application.”
